Print sizes, resolution & permitted uses

Every PDF includes US Letter, US Legal, US Tabloid, Half Letter, A3, A4, A5, A6, 8 × 10, 11 × 14 and 12 × 18 inches in both portrait and landscape, plus 6 × 6, 8 × 8 and 12 × 12-inch square pages.

The full texture is centered and fitted proportionally, with clean unused space and a minimum ¼-inch margin. Choose the matching PDF page and print at Actual Size / 100%; use Fit to Printable Area if your printer needs wider margins.

The original artwork is 1024 × 1536 pixels. PNG canvases are 1032 × 1544 pixels, including a narrow transparent border; the artwork itself stays opaque. Larger print layouts use the same source detail and are not native 300-PPI artwork. Smaller prints will appear sharper.

Personal use and commercial use in finished creative projects are permitted. The source textures may not be redistributed, shared or resold as standalone assets. See the included license for details.
