Region Notes and Geography Studies for Worldbuilding

Not every fantasy map begins as finished cartography. Regional notes are often where the world becomes coherent: climate, terrain, settlements, hazards, borders, resources, and travel constraints can be worked out before the final lines are inked. This field note uses a Mapmaker Sheets surface to show how an aged page can support both rough thinking and presentable worldbuilding records.

Region planning is the unglamorous little engine behind convincing maps. Before a polished map exists, there are questions about terrain, settlement patterns, trade, distance, danger, and why a road or border exists where it does.

A dedicated sheet gives those notes somewhere to live beside sketches. That makes it easier to see contradictions early, while the map is still cheap to change.

The aged-paper treatment keeps the planning documents visually connected to the finished world. It turns development notes into something you can keep, print, or hand across a gaming table without losing the atmosphere.
