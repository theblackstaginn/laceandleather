Suneen Reef: Fantasy Map Showcase

Suneen Reef is presented as a Lace & Leather fantasy cartography showcase, pairing illustrated geography with the aged, archival atmosphere that runs through the studio’s map work. The piece is a useful reference for worldbuilders thinking about how place names, coastlines, water, landmarks, and weathered material can work together without turning a map into decorative clutter.

Suneen Reef sits inside Lace & Leather’s fantasy-cartography side of the workshop: mapmaking that aims to feel like an object from the world rather than a clean diagram dropped on top of it.

For worldbuilders, that means thinking about more than geography alone. Surface, age, labeling, contrast, and the rhythm of empty space all help decide whether a map reads as a believable artifact while still remaining useful at the table or on the page.

This showcase is here as a visual reference for that balance. The goal is atmosphere with function intact: enough weathering to carry history, enough clarity to keep the world legible.
